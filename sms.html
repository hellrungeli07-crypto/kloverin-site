Kloverin SMS Program

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Kloverin sends text messages to people who ask us to, such as a booking link after a call, a confirmation that we received a request, or a follow-up about a question they asked. We only text people who have agreed to receive messages, and we never text marketing to anyone who hasn't opted in.

How you opt in

You can agree to receive texts from Kloverin in any of these ways:

  • Online form: entering your mobile number on our website and checking the consent box (example below).
  • On a phone call: calling our number and saying "yes" when our assistant asks, "Can I text you a link to book?"
  • By texting us first: sending a text to our number. We reply only to that conversation.

Consent is never a condition of purchase.

What we send

Booking links, confirmations that we received your call or request, appointment reminders, and replies to your questions. Message frequency varies based on your requests, typically 1 to 5 messages per inquiry.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email contact@kloverin.com.

Costs and carriers

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
