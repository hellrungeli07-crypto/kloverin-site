Check these first

  1. ThermostatSet to Cool, a few degrees below room temperature. Replace the batteries if the screen is blank or dim.
  2. BreakerFind the AC and air handler breakers in your panel. If one has tripped, reset it once. If it trips again, leave it off and call.
  3. Air filterA filter packed with dust can shut airflow down and freeze the coil. Swap it if it's gray.
  4. Ice on the lines?Turn the system to Off and the fan to On to thaw it. Running a frozen unit can damage the compressor.
  5. Drain lineMany systems shut off when the condensate drain backs up. If there's water in the drain pan, that's likely it.

Stay safe in the heat

Close blinds on the sunny side, run ceiling fans, drink water, and check on older neighbors and pets. If anyone feels dizzy, confused or stops sweating, call 911.
