Questions

Do I pay before I see the site?

No. We build it first and send you a preview link. You only pay once you're happy with it.

I don't have a domain. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We'll help you pick one and set it up. A domain usually costs about $10 to $20 a year.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It's yours. If you ever leave the care plan, we'll hand over the files so you can host it anywhere.

Where do quote requests go?

Straight to your email, with the customer's name, phone number, the service they need and a description of the job.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. On the care plan, just text or email us what you'd like changed. Without it, changes are billed per request.

How long does it take?

You'll usually see your preview within a few days. Once you approve it, going live takes a day or two.

Want to see yours?

Send us your business name and we'll build a free preview. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.
