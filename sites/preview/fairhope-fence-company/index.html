Fences & gates

What we build and fix

Every job starts with Jack coming out to look at the yard, talk through options and layout, and send you an estimate.

Privacy fences

Tall wood privacy runs, including a 300+ foot fence and full back-yard jobs.

Shadow box

Boards on both sides, so the fence looks finished from your yard and your neighbor’s.

Picket fences

Front-yard and pool pickets, including a picket fence around a pool with a locking gate.

Ranch rail

Open rail fencing for driveways, pastures and big lots, like a 3-acre back yard.

Pool & metal fencing

Black metal fencing around pools and yards, and pool fences with locking gates.

Gates & repairs

Double gates, gate repairs, reset posts and rebuilt sections, even after a storm.

More than fences: customers have also hired Jack’s crew for a front porch rebuild, an accent wall, a painted brick floor and other home repairs. Ask about your project.
