What we handle

Inground pool repairs, renovations & new construction.

Every service here comes from a real Premier Choice job, from a single pump swap to reviving a 40 year old pool.

Vinyl liner replacement

Tara liners in current, last-season and closeout designs, set with a liner vacuum.

Renovation, replaster & tile

Plaster resurfacing, new waterline tile and full renovations, residential and commercial.

Floor, wall & step repair

Full bottom repairs, rotten wall boards replaced and fiberglass steps repaired.

Equipment

Pumps and booster pumps, filters and sand changes, laterals, multiport valves, salt systems and pool lights.

Plumbing & skimmers

Pipe breaks under the deck and skimmer replacement, with the concrete deck patched to match.

Green-to-clean

Inspections & pool school

Pool inspections for home buyers, and a walk-through "pool school" for new owners.

New construction
