Is your panel keeping up?

Homes built before 2000 often run today's kitchens, AC and chargers on panels made for half the load. Call us if any of these sound familiar.

  • Breakers trip when the AC and dryer run together
  • The panel is warm to the touch or buzzes
  • The label says Federal Pacific or Zinsco
  • You're adding an EV charger, hot tub or new room
