Your lawn's year

What we do through the seasons, so your yard stays green from the first warm week in March.

  1. March to May

    Wake up

    Pre-emergent before crabgrass sprouts, first feed and the season's first cut.

  2. June to August

    Beat the heat

    Taller cuts to shade the roots, grub control and sprinkler checks.

  3. September to November

    Recover

    Aeration, overseeding where it's thin, and leaf cleanup.

  4. December to February

    Rest

    Winter weed control and a mow when it needs it. You only pay when we come.
