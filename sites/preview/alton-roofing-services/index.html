What we do

Every kind of roof job, start to finish.

Same crew, same standards, whether it's a few missing shingles before a hurricane or a full tear-off on the family home.

Roof replacement

Full tear-off and new roof. You pick the shingles, drip edge and ventilation. We handle permits, materials and cleanup.

Roof repairs

Leaks, missing shingles, flashing and storm wear. We come out quick for an estimate and get it fixed fast.

Shingle roofs

Architectural shingles in the color you want, installed clean, with the yard and driveway picked up when we leave.

Metal roofs

Long-lasting metal for homes and outbuildings. Ask us whether metal makes sense for your house and budget.

Storm damage & insurance

We don't leave you alone with the insurance company.

Jacksonville weather is hard on roofs. When a storm does the damage, we document it, help you through the claim, and stick with you until the roof is replaced, even when it takes a while.
