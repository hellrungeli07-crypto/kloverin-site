What we do

Homes, shops and businesses.

From a dead outlet to a full rewire on a house built in 1898, we'll figure out what's wrong, explain it plainly, and quote it before we start.

Troubleshooting & repair

Tripped breakers, dead circuits, flickering lights, bad connections. We find the cause, not just the symptom.

Panel upgrades

Panel replacements, sub panels, moving panels and outdoor breaker boxes. Usually done in a day, often by noon.

Generators

Whole-home Generac installs with propane setup, transfer switches and generator outlets, before the next storm hits.

Service poles & hookups

New poles, meter bases and bringing service up to code. We work with SLEMCO and Entergy so your power gets turned back on.

Lighting, fans & outlets

Chandeliers, ceiling fans, soffit and flood lights, new outlets, dishwashers and stoves wired right.

Sheds, shops & outdoor kitchens

Trenching and running power out back, wiring sheds, workshops, patios and outdoor kitchens.

Got a Federal Pacific panel?

Those panels have been linked to serious safety concerns, and homeowner's insurance companies are starting to require them to be replaced at renewal. Not sure what's in your house? We'll check.
