The work

What the crew takes on

"Tree Trimming, Tree Removal, Stump Grinding, Ornamental Pruning." Plus brush clearing, storm cleanup and more, for homes and businesses.

01

Tree removal

Large and hazardous trees over houses, beside fences and driveways, and near power and gas lines. Dropped where Tim says, or taken down in pieces.

02

Trimming and canopy raising

Pruning big oaks back off the roof and raising the canopy so the yard gets light. "Trimming trees can prevent catastrophic failure."

03

Ornamental pruning

Careful shaping of the ornamental trees around your home.

04

Stump grinding

Stumps ground down after the removal, as part of the same job.

05

Brush and land clearing

Overgrown lots cleared of brush and trees. The grapple truck loads the heavy wood.

06

Storm cleanup

Trees on roofs and across yards after hurricanes and wind storms, from Sally to Helene.

07

Crane and bucket truck work

A crane with a 120 foot reach for trees that can't come down over the house, and bucket trucks for high limbs.

Firewood

Start to finish

How a removal goes

Four steps, each one described in customer reviews. You get one crew from the quote to the last pile of leaves.

  1. 1

    The quote

    Tim looks at the tree, the house, the lines and the fence, and tells you what he'll do and what it costs.

    "Gave us the quote, came the next day, removed one huge tree, took out the stump and trimmed several others and cleaned up."Carol R., from her Google review
  2. 2

    The cut

    The crew rigs it, notches it and brings it down where it was planned, or lowers it in pieces when there's no room.

    "They got right to work and dropped the tree exactly where Tim said he would with no mess and no damage."Jerry S., from his Google review
  3. 3

    The stump

    If the stump is part of the job, it gets ground out on schedule.

    "And yes, this company actually removes your stumps when they say they will!"Rachel B., from her Google review
  4. 4

    The cleanup

    Limbs, logs and debris are loaded and hauled off, and the yard gets raked and blown clean.

    "They left my yard with 0 signs a huge crepe Myrtle was there before. Cleaned up all debris and hauled it away."Liz R., from her Google review
