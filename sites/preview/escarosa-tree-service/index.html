What EscaRosa does

One tree or a whole fence line. Every job starts with a free estimate.

Tree removal

Big oaks, tall pines and dead or leaning trees, including the ones right next to the house, the fence or the power lines.

Trimming & shaping

Live oaks, magnolias, crepe myrtles and palms trimmed back off the roof and shaped the way you want them.

Stump grinding

Stumps ground down so the yard is level again, old ones included.

Storm & emergency removal

Trees on the roof, across the driveway or blown over after a hurricane, cut up and cleared.

Clearing & cleanup

Overgrown fence lines, vines, old bushes and debris piles cleared out and hauled off.

Haul-off

Limbs, logs and debris taken with them. Or keep a stack of firewood if you want it.
