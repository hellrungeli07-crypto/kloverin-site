How a fence goes up

Old fence out, posts in. Then boards, gates and cleanup.

That's how customer after customer describes their job: torn down and posts set on day one, finished on day two, yard left clean.

Day one

Tear-out and posts

The old fence comes down and gets hauled off, and the new posts go in on your line.

“Our fence was torn down and posts up first day.”Alan K., Google review

Day two

Boards, gates, cleanup

Rails and boards go up, gates get hung and adjusted, and the job site gets cleaned before the crew leaves.
