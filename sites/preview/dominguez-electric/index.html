Services

What we fix and install

Homes and businesses across San Antonio. If it runs on electricity and it isn't working right, call.

Breakers & panels

Tripping breakers, worn-out breakers, panel problems and undersized disconnects replaced the right way.

Ceiling fans

New fans assembled, mounted and balanced so they don't wobble, including patio fans and attic work.

Lighting

Chandeliers, fixtures, security and flood lights, step lights and commercial lighting.

Troubleshooting

Flickering lights, dead rooms and short circuits traced to the real cause, even in old or odd wiring.

Outlets & switches

Outlet, switch and GFCI repair, plus dryer and stove plugs that stopped working.

Emergency & storm repair

Bath vents & heaters

Home warranty repairs
