Services

From the meter to the faucet.

Repairs, installations and maintenance. Every service below is a job their customers have written about or photographed.

Water heaters

Tank and tankless, gas and electric. New installs and replacements in closets and utility rooms, tankless units mounted outside, and tankless service.

Repiping

Whole-home and bathroom repipes, replacing old poly tubing with new lines, including older homes in East Hill.

Gas lines & appliances

New gas lines from the meter to the kitchen and laundry room, plus gas range, oven and dryer hookups.

"Inspector raved about how well the installation was done by Spencer and Trent. Passed with flying colors!"Tom K., from his Google review

Leaks & broken pipes

Tracking down leaks, repairing broken pipes and getting the water back on.

Drains & sewer

Clogged sewer drains, slow kitchen sinks, sewer cleanouts, grinder pumps and lift stations.

Toilets

New toilets set and running toilets repaired.

Faucets, fixtures & tub valves

Kitchen and bath faucets, sink fixtures and under-sink plumbing, garbage disposals, shower heads, and old tub valves converted to a single handle.

Outdoor showers & spigots

Outdoor shower kits installed, and outdoor spigots relocated, even from one brick wall to another.
