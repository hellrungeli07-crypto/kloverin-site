Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number. We give you a dedicated Kloverin line, and your existing number forwards to it when you don't pick up (or all the time, if you'd like the AI to answer first).

Will it sound like a robot?

It holds a natural back-and-forth conversation and answers in your business's name. The best way to judge is to call the demo line yourself.

What happens if it can't answer something?

If a caller asks for a person or the AI isn't sure, it passes the call to you or texts the caller to follow up. Either way, you get their details by text.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days. We need your services, hours, common questions and a booking link. We handle the rest, including the phone number and carrier registration.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. Cancel any time.

Do I need to manage any software?

No. Kloverin is done for you. We set it up, monitor it and keep improving it. If you want something changed, just tell us.
